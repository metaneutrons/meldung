# Reporting Data Protection and IT Security Incidents

Use this portal to report security incidents and personal data breaches to the responsible units of Hochschule Hannover.

> **Do you have a technical problem?** A forgotten password, trouble with Wi-Fi, a printer or your e-mail client, a software request or a service that is down do not belong here. You will find guides, current outages and how to contact support on the [Hochschul-IT service pages](https://service.it.hs-hannover.de/). This portal is solely for security and data protection incidents. Technical issues are not handled here.

## What should you report here?

- **Unauthorised access** to systems, accounts or data, including a mere suspicion. This includes sign-ins you did not make yourself.
- **Phishing and malware**, especially if you clicked a suspicious link, entered credentials or opened an attachment, or if a device is infected with a virus or ransomware.
- **Loss or theft** of devices and storage media (laptop, smartphone, USB stick) holding work-related or personal data.
- **Data breaches**, for example confidential information sent to the wrong recipients, or data that was accidentally accessible to unauthorised people.
- **Signs of an attack**, such as files that are suddenly encrypted or altered, unknown programs or user accounts.

If in doubt, report it. If a problem might be connected to an attack or to personal data, it belongs here.

## Important notes

- **Report quickly.** The sooner we know, the better we can limit the damage. As a rule, the university must notify personal data breaches to the data protection supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of them (Art. 33 GDPR). Our assessment has to fit into that time as well, so please report right away, even if not everything is clear yet.
- **Your progress is saved.** Your entries are stored automatically in your browser. You can stop at any time and continue later.
- **Don't forget to submit.** We only receive your report once you submit it. You can skip steps and add missing details later.

## How to proceed

1. **Fill in the form.** Give as much detail as you have. Required fields are marked, but incomplete information helps us too.
2. **Submit.** Finish with "Submit", even if you cannot answer every question.
3. **Keep the reference number.** After submitting you receive a reference number and can download your report as a PDF.
4. **Stay reachable.** Please be available for follow-up questions through the contact details you provided.
